## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $4,000 a month (Fundador (coste/sueldo, ajustable) $2,000, Marketing de adquisicion/mes (estimado) $1,500, Infra base + herramientas + servicios $500).

- **Break-even: 1,633 suscripcion/mess a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (3,000 a day): **28%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 100000 a day.
